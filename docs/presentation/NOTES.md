Illustrative business scenario only (USD; not validated or current vendor rates):
price 150/institution/month, 3,000 answers/institution/month, variable cost
0.005/answer, shared hosting 20/month, shared support allowance 80/month.
Variable expense is 15/institution/month. With one institution, monthly surplus
is 35 before tax, salaries, acquisition, and other unmodeled costs. Recurring
break-even is ceil(100/(150-15)) = 1 institution under these assumptions.
A 300 initial allowance would take 300/35 = 8.57 months to recover at one client.
These assumptions omit substantial real operating risks and are not a validated
business case. The slides show hypothetical point-in-time institution counts
at 12/24/36 months, not contracted customers or recognized revenue forecasts.
